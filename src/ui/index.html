You're set

  • When the call starts, press Listen (). macOS asks for screen and audio access the first time: allow it, then reopen Dialpal once, since macOS only sends audio after a reopen.
  • In Sales mode, Dialpal shows your reply on its own once the prospect finishes talking. Any time, you can press Answer () or type a question.
  • Everything here lives in Settings, the gear at the top.
New call block? Keep the same product brief, or change it.
